About the curve & the measurements +

The playing arc is made first with flat vertical sides. The underside is then carved up to meet those sides smoothly. Finally, a small circular rounding removes the sharp top corner. Set corner drop to zero to see the unsoftened corner. The rounding radius is calculated from the drop. The underside is completely independent of this finishing cut. The current construction preset retains a nominal flat side of 10% of the centre thickness before rounding.

The Meares overlays are digitized drawing outlines scaled to your chosen width. The presets are interpretations, not exact reproductions. The originals have no physical scale. The underside template follows the lower half of the curve, including the carved transitions into the sides, and adds a 20 mm backing band. The label is vector geometry with bridges through enclosed letter counters. Some combinations cannot form a convex profile with a flatter playing surface; adjust the controls if a message appears.

SVG dimensions are in millimetres. Print at 100% and check the width before using a template. The preview is scaled to fit your screen. All calculations and downloads happen on your device.
