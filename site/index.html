About the curve & the measurements +

This follows how the board is made. A rectangular blank is radiused on top; the underside is then worked down until it rises to meet the blank’s original flat face; the flat left over is softened last with a file. The underside is a single superellipse, which reaches that flat face with a vertical tangent the way the wood does. Set corner rounding to zero to see the unsoftened corner. The rounding radius is calculated from it, and the underside is completely independent of this finishing cut. How much flat each design leaves is a property of that design, not a fixed proportion.

The Meares overlays are digitized drawing outlines scaled to your chosen width. The presets are interpretations, not exact reproductions. The originals have no physical scale. The underside template follows the lower half of the curve right up to the flat sides, and adds a 20 mm backing band. The label is vector geometry with bridges through enclosed letter counters. Some combinations leave no depth below the flat sides; adjust the controls if a message appears.

SVG dimensions are in millimetres. Print at 100% and check the width before using a template. The preview is scaled to fit your screen. All calculations and downloads happen on your device.
